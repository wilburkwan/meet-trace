## Name
Meet Trace

## Summary (max 132 characters)
Live captions with free on-device translation for Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, plus meeting history and YouTube transcripts.

## Category
Productivity › Communication

## Detailed description

Can't keep up in a meeting held in another language? Meet Trace translates Google Meet and Microsoft Teams captions in real time and keeps a complete record of every line.

Translation runs on your own computer with Chrome's built-in translator. It's free and needs no sign-up and no API key, and your meeting never leaves your device.

DURING THE MEETING
• Floating caption window that you can drag, resize or minimize, so it never covers the call
• Turns on captions (CC) for you when you join (Google Meet and Teams on the web)
• Live translation shown side by side with the original, updated while people are still talking
• Grouped by speaker, like Google Meet; or split by pauses if you prefer
• Hover a sentence to highlight it in both the original and the translation
• Take notes during the meeting
• "Copy entire transcript" in one click, ready to paste into ChatGPT or any AI assistant

AFTER THE MEETING
• Every meeting is saved automatically, grouped by date and searchable by speaker or text
• Captions with translations, plus chat and notes, in one place
• Export to CSV or TXT, and back up or restore everything as JSON

YOUTUBE TRANSCRIPTS
On any YouTube video, click the Meet Trace icon to read the full transcript with timestamps. Copy it (optionally with a translation prompt for AI) or download it as TXT or SRT.

MAKE IT YOURS
• 20 interface languages that follow your system language by default
• 4 colour themes
• Adjustable caption size, segment style and translation update frequency

PRIVACY
Captions, translations, notes and settings are stored only in your own browser. Meet Trace has no servers and collects no data.

REQUIREMENTS
• Desktop Google Chrome 138 or later (for Chrome's built-in translator)
• Microsoft Teams must be opened in Chrome (the desktop app can't run extensions)
• Download the translation model once per language pair from the settings page

Meet Trace is an independent project and is not affiliated with Google, Microsoft or YouTube.
