# Meet Trace 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

最後更新 / Last updated：2026-10-07

## 繁體中文

Meet Trace（以下稱「本擴充功能」）重視你的隱私。

**我們蒐集哪些資料？**
不蒐集。本擴充功能沒有任何伺服器，也不會把你的資料傳送給開發者或任何第三方。

**本擴充功能在你的電腦上處理哪些資料？**
- 會議字幕、發言人名稱、聊天訊息（Google Meet）、你輸入的筆記，以及會議標題與時間。
- YouTube 影片的逐字稿（僅在你點擊「讀取逐字稿」時讀取）。
- 你的設定（介面語言、配色、翻譯語言等）。

以上資料只儲存在你的瀏覽器（`chrome.storage.local`）中，你可以隨時在「會議紀錄」頁刪除，或移除本擴充功能一併清除。

**翻譯**
翻譯使用 Chrome 內建的翻譯功能，在你的電腦上執行，不會把內容傳到外部伺服器。

**你主動分享的內容**
當你按下「用 ChatGPT 摘要」時，本擴充功能會開啟 chatgpt.com 並帶入該場會議的內容；當你使用「複製」功能時，內容會放到你的剪貼簿。這些動作只會在你主動點擊時發生，之後的處理適用該服務自身的隱私權政策。

**權限用途**
- 讀取 Google Meet、Microsoft Teams 與 YouTube 頁面內容：用於擷取字幕、顯示字幕視窗、讀取逐字稿。
- 儲存空間：在本機保存設定與會議紀錄。
- 目前分頁（activeTab）：讓工具列視窗知道目前是否為 YouTube 影片或會議分頁。

**聯絡我們**
如有任何問題，請至 https://github.com/wilburkwan/meet-trace/issues 提出。

## English

Meet Trace ("the extension") respects your privacy.

**What data do we collect?**
None. The extension has no servers and never sends your data to the developer or any third party.

**What data does the extension process on your computer?**
- Meeting captions, speaker names, chat messages (Google Meet), the notes you type, and meeting titles and times.
- YouTube video transcripts, read only when you click "Read transcript".
- Your settings, such as interface language, theme and translation languages.

This data is stored only in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`). You can delete it at any time from the Meeting History page, or by removing the extension.

**Translation**
Translation uses Chrome's built-in translator and runs on your computer. Content is never sent to an external server.

**Content you choose to share**
When you click "Summarize in ChatGPT", the extension opens chatgpt.com with that meeting's content. When you use a copy action, the content goes to your clipboard. These happen only when you click, and the receiving service's own privacy policy applies afterwards.

**Permissions**
- Read Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and YouTube pages: to capture captions, show the caption window and read transcripts.
- Storage: to keep settings and meeting history locally.
- activeTab: so the toolbar popup can tell whether the current tab is a YouTube video or a meeting.

**Contact**
Please open an issue at https://github.com/wilburkwan/meet-trace/issues.
